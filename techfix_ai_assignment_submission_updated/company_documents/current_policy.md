1. Change-of-mind returns
An unused, standard, unopened product may be requested for return within 14 calendar days after delivery. The customer must provide the order confirmation or receipt, and the product must be in its original packaging with included accessories. Support reviews the request before a return is authorized. This rule is a return eligibility window; it does not promise a cash refund amount, payment method, or processing time. Do not describe a return request as approved until support confirms it.

2. Defective or damaged items
Report visible shipping damage within 48 hours after delivery and include the order number and clear photographs of the outer package and item. For a suspected product defect discovered after opening, contact support within 7 calendar days after delivery with the order number, model, and a short description. Support acknowledges a complete defect report within 2 business days. The team may ask for additional details or refer the customer to a manufacturer service center. These steps do not guarantee replacement, repair, or a refund.

3. Exceptions
Exception A: a custom-configured CCTV bundle cannot be returned for a change of mind once configuration has begun, because components may already have been selected and paired. This exception does not remove the instruction to report a defective or damaged delivered item through the defect process.

Exception B: if severe weather or a road closure prevents a scheduled on-site visit, the visit may be rescheduled without a rescheduling fee. The customer should contact support to select a new time. This exception applies to the rescheduling fee only; it does not promise a particular replacement slot or compensation.

4. Appointments
For a normal customer-requested change to an on-site appointment, give at least 24 hours' notice. Changes with shorter notice may incur a PKR 1,000 rescheduling fee. The severe-weather/road-closure exception above takes precedence when it applies. The remote setup consultation is a separate listed service; product return rules do not automatically cancel or refund a booked service.

5. Support and records
The support desk is open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time, and closed Sundays and public holidays. Include an order number for an order-specific request, but do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment card numbers, or private account credentials. Staff do not need remote access to a customer's computer for the services described here.

6. Safe technical support
Support may help locate a published manual, identify an error code from a visible display, and explain a non-invasive observation in the operations guide. Stop using equipment if smoke, sparks, burning odor, exposed wires, water near powered equipment, or repeated breaker trips are present, if it is safe to stop. Keep away from exposed parts and contact a qualified professional. Do not open energized equipment, bypass protective devices, handle refrigerant, or climb to an unsafe mounting point. These are safety boundaries, not a complete repair procedure.

7. Selecting policy versions
Version 2.0 is the current rule set from 2026-11-01 onward. Version 1.0 is archived and is only evidence of the former rule. When a user asks for the current rule, use version 2.0 even if an archived passage ranks highly. When asked to compare or describe the old rule, name version 1.0 as archived and explain the change. If a retrieved result contains both versions, state which one governs now and cite both only when the question asks about the difference. If the question asks for a detail neither version supplies, say the documents do not provide it.
