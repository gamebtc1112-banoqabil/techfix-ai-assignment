Historical scope
This archived policy records the former rules for the fictional TechFix Supply & Service. It is not the rule to apply to a new request after 2026-10-31. The current policy version 2.0 became effective 2026-11-01. Use this file when a customer asks what the earlier policy said, or when comparing policy changes. Do not present a version 1.0 rule as current.

Former change-of-mind return window
Under version 1.0, an unused, unopened standard product could be requested for return within 7 calendar days after delivery. The customer had to show an order confirmation or receipt, and the item had to remain in original packaging with accessories. A request still required support review and authorization. The former policy did not state a guaranteed cash amount, payment method, or processing time.

Former defect reporting window
Under version 1.0, a suspected defect or delivery damage had to be reported within 3 calendar days after delivery with an order number, product model, and a description. Support aimed to acknowledge a complete report within 5 business days. Acknowledgment was not a promise of replacement, repair, or refund.

Former appointment rescheduling rule
Version 1.0 asked customers to provide 48 hours' notice for a normal requested change to an on-site appointment. A shorter notice could incur a PKR 1,000 fee. The former policy did not include the later severe-weather/road-closure fee exception. The current policy describes that exception separately.

Comparison guide
The return request window changed from 7 to 14 calendar days. The defect report deadline changed from 3 to 7 calendar days, while acknowledgment changed from 5 to 2 business days. Normal appointment notice changed from 48 to 24 hours; version 2.0 added the severe-weather fee exception.

The current policy's custom-configured bundle exception and visible shipping damage reporting window are described in version 2.0. This archive does not say that those later rules existed under version 1.0. Do not infer a historical condition from a later policy.

Version selection reminder
For a current return question, use version 2.0 (14 days); for the former rule, use archived version 1.0 (7 days). A retrieved archive never overrides the current rule. Neither version provides a guaranteed refund amount or method.
